Springtime in MIchigan

springtimeThe challenge with a Michigan spring is what to wear. One day it may be 70 degrees, and the next day it may be 30 degrees. It is also known to swing in temperature, which could cause extinction in some critters. I often start out with a warm coat, hat & gloves. By mid-morning, I take off the hat & gloves and switch to a light spring jacket
